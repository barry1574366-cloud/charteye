What is a Candlestick?
A candlestick shows the price movement of a time period. One candle = one story of buyers vs sellers.
Bull
Bear
2
Anatomy of a Candle
A candle has 3 parts: the body, the upper shadow (wick), and the lower shadow (wick).
Upper Shadow Body Lower Shadow
3
Green = Up, Red = Down
Green candle: Close price is higher than Open price. Buyers won this round.

Red candle: Close price is lower than Open price. Sellers won this round.
4
Body & Shadow Meaning
Body = the price range between open and close. A big body means strong momentum.

Upper shadow = the highest price reached. Long upper shadow means buyers tried but failed.

Lower shadow = the lowest price reached. Long lower shadow means sellers tried but failed.
